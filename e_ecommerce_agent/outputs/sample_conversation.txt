Sample run of run_demo.py against Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash). Tool calls are shown as [tool].
The audit trail for the two writes (prepare_order, confirm_order) is in outputs/audit.log (git-ignored).

>>> CUSTOMER: Hi! I'm looking for a waterproof hiking backpack, budget around $150.
    [tool] search-products({'max_price': '150', 'keyword': 'backpack', 'category': ''})
    [tool] search-products({'category': '', 'keyword': 'waterproof', 'max_price': '150'})
    [tool] search-products({'category': 'Backpacks', 'max_price': '150', 'keyword': ''})
